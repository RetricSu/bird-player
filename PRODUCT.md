# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who maintain and listen to a local music library on desktop, including music collected from authorized online sources. They want a compact, dependable player for everyday use, with familiar library, playlist, metadata, lyrics, and subtitle workflows.

## Product Purpose

Bird Player is a serious daily-use desktop music player with a nostalgic early-2000s character. It organizes local audio, manages playlists and metadata, downloads authorized audio, and keeps useful playback data such as artwork, lyrics, and timed subtitles close to the media.

## Brand Personality

Nostalgic, compact, practical.

## Anti-references

No specific product or visual anti-reference is required. Avoid decorative SaaS patterns, card-heavy layouts, gratuitous motion, and unfamiliar controls that interfere with the feel of a focused native desktop music player.

## Design Principles

1. Keep playback and library tasks immediately understandable.
2. Preserve the compact information density of a classic desktop player.
3. Store portable media data with the audio whenever the format permits.
4. Reuse shared behavior while keeping different media concepts semantically distinct.
5. Add personality through the product's existing visual language, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Apply normal desktop-product accessibility practices: readable contrast, clear active states, keyboard-compatible controls where supported by the toolkit, and motion limited to communicating playback or state changes. No additional compliance target has been specified.
